BID ME COME.

A single by The Producer of Tracks. Its title comes from Peter’s words to Jesus in the storm: “bid me come unto thee on the water” (Matthew 14:28).

The storm behind the song

This crossing follows the feeding of the crowd. Jesus sends the disciples ahead and withdraws to pray.

…the ship was now in the midst of the sea, tossed with waves: for the wind was contrary.
Matthew 14:24 · KJV
Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.
Matthew 14:27 · KJV
Lord, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water. And he said, Come.
Matthew 14:28-29 · KJV
…beginning to sink, he cried, saying, Lord, save me. And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him…
Matthew 14:30-31 · KJV
And when they were come into the ship, the wind ceased. Then they that were in the ship came and worshipped him…
Matthew 14:32-33 · KJV

Scripture: King James Version. Short extracts are marked with an ellipsis. KJV source
